# Kalshi Market Lab

**Kalshi Market Lab | Python, SQL, JavaScript, React, Next.js, FastAPI, Excel | Independent Project**

- Built an interactive prediction-market research platform with Python/FastAPI and server-side JavaScript, refreshing Kalshi prices, order-book depth and trade data across five market verticals every 30 seconds with explicit freshness and coverage controls.
- Engineered Python/SQL research pipelines with DuckDB dimensions and facts, point-in-time evaluation joins and Parquet exports; developed contract-specific evidence briefs connecting resolution rules to TSA, National Weather Service, FRED and Coinbase data.
- Built browser-local paper trading with partial fills, fee-inclusive cost basis, bid-based exits and realized/unrealized P/L; delivered Excel/CSV exports and validated research, execution and accounting behavior through 123 JavaScript/Python automated tests.
