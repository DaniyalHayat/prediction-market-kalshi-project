# Selected-contract evidence

Updated November 6, 2026 ET.

## Contract and source boundaries

`contractEvidence(market, {enabled, selectedTicker, redditEnabled, includeX, news, sports, music})` requires explicit enablement and an exact selected ticker before any new retrieval. Reddit remains a separate opt-in. The result supplies ranked items, readable summary and long-read views, source status, film preference sensitivity and music alternatives. Publisher headlines and social posts have zero direct forecast weight. The selected NBA evidence route also reports any structured fixture input used by the paper screen.

Items retain source URLs, dates, headline/description scope, engagement metadata where supplied, source tiers and reasons for relevance. ESPN descriptions, publisher headlines and public X posts are not represented as full article reads. GDELT first-seen timestamps remain labeled as first-seen metadata. Editorial ranking weights are entity relevance 40%, source tier 25%, recency 20% and specific update terms 15%. These are review-order choices, not validated source accuracy or causal price-impact estimates. Duplicated titles are removed and publisher diversity is promoted; syndication and correlated reporting remain possible.

Recent discovery is not recent publication. Explicit historical dates in a headline or dated article path are screened out of current news even when RSS or a first-seen provider reports today's timestamp. A sports game-details/preview/recap page must match both selected teams; a page for Washington–Charlotte cannot qualify for an Atlanta–Washington trade just because it names Washington. Actual last-five and head-to-head history remains available separately as historical sports evidence. Retained search rejects explicitly historical news and each new evidence load atomically replaces the selected ticker's current public chunk snapshot, so previously rejected context does not remain searchable.

The curated source catalog records identity evidence for [Shams Charania at ESPN](https://us.espnpressroom.com/bios/shams-charania/) and [Fabrizio Romano at CBS Sports](https://www.paramountpressexpress.com/cbs-sports/shows/soccer/talent/?view=fabrizio-romano-2). This establishes reporting roles and topic scope, not a measured forecast record. Official league sources, ESPN, film trade publications and third-party music-chart commentators have different roles. The chart commentators are not official Spotify sources. No source is certified accurate based on follower count or reputation.

## Access and request controls

- Public source hosts are explicitly allowlisted. Responses have byte limits, deadlines and no redirect following. Requests use a shared cache and deduplicate in-flight work. Provider 429 responses honor Retry-After and X reset timestamps; authentication failures and server failures trigger backoff.
- X uses the [public account-post timeline](https://docs.x.com/x-api/users/get-posts), never private communications. At most two curated accounts are read per topic, cached for 30 minutes with a cursor. Before a cold request, the app reserves five possible post reads and account-lookup costs against durable shared daily, monthly-post and monetary allowances. At review time [published pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing) was $0.005 per public post and $0.010 per user resource. Defaults are 1,000 reserved post reads and $6.50 in combined read reservations per month. Provider billing can change, and reservations are conservative application controls rather than an invoice guarantee. No live paid X requests were issued for this lane's verification.
- Reddit's [official FAQ](https://developers.reddit.com/docs/guides/faq) distinguishes Devvit apps running on Reddit from external Data API applications. Devvit login does not issue external OAuth credentials. Approved external OAuth access can be supplied with `REDDIT_DATA_API_APPROVED`, `REDDIT_ACCESS_TOKEN` and an identifying `REDDIT_USER_AGENT`.
- The user requested public-page collection as an alternative. The fallback checks robots rules before one bounded public JSON-page request. Reddit's live robots file returned `User-agent: *` and `Disallow: /`, so the collector returned `robots_blocked` and did not request the listing. The UI can expose a direct public search link. A CAPTCHA, 403 or 429 stops retrieval; there is no proxy rotation, auth bypass or alternate-host retry.
- A successful Reddit request examines at most 25 public link posts from one topic subreddit, cached 15 minutes across viewers. No comments or author-history calls are made. Missing/deleted content, likely automation, vote score below 3, upvote ratio below 65%, self-posts, overt opinions/promotions and duplicates are excluded. These are fallible heuristics; surviving posts are not certified human or factual. Rate and quantity limits are shared across viewers. Social reads fail closed if a durable quota/cursor store is unavailable.

## Music: diagnosis and actual fallback

The active Apple endpoint `rss.marketingtools.apple.com` returned 100 US song rows with a dated feed. The legacy `rss.applemarketingtools.com` hostname failed retrieval and has been removed from the provider allowlist. The supplied backend Spotify credentials were checked in memory with one client-credentials request and one public artist search; both returned HTTP 200. No credentials or tokens were written to the repository. This demonstrates local credential capability, not production environment configuration.

An available Spotify catalog is not a Spotify artist-stream chart. Billboard song outcomes are not used as artist search queries. [Spotify's search reference](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/reference/search) describes catalog search and policy restrictions. Spotify catalog references remain display-only and are excluded from AI packets and models. The [July 2026 development-mode update](https://developer.spotify.com/blog/2026-07-23-web-api-quota-updates) states that quotas are shared per developer account and 429 bodies can distinguish `QUOTA_EXCEEDED`. It supersedes earlier app-count guidance; do not infer current access solely from an old migration guide.

A working additional adapter reads [Kworb's US daily Spotify song chart](https://kworb.net/spotify/country/us_daily.html). Its robots rules allowed public collection. The bounded parser verified 200 unique song IDs/ranks, US territory, an explicit 2026-11-03 measurement date and reported daily song-stream counts. Exact artist/song matches are exposed, with the source labeled a secondary chart mirror. This is materially useful streaming context for music questions, but it is neither all-artist US totals nor a Billboard chart. No truncated top-200 sum is presented as an artist total. Official [Spotify Charts](https://charts.spotify.com/charts/overview/us), [Billboard Hot 100](https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/) and [YouTube Charts](https://charts.youtube.com/) remain direct review links. Billboard's robots rules disallow GPT agents, so the adapter does not disguise its identity to collect that chart.

## NBA evidence

Matched sports research includes each team's last five completed games and form, the last five observed head-to-head games, recent player points/rebounds/assists/minutes and actual dated boxscore rows. Missing players or boxscores stay missing; an explicit DNP row has zero observed minutes. Player averages use observed statistics and expose coverage. Injury snapshots are current reported availability, not a reconstructed historical injury archive. For official participation status consult the dated [NBA injury reports](https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season), which the league updates throughout the day.

Low-minute comparisons show observed results, including sample sizes and intervals. They describe games in which coaching, foul trouble, game state, rest and injuries can all affect minutes. Retrieval is bounded to 10 recent team games, or 24 when availability comparisons are requested, with concurrency three and week-long completed-boxscore caching.

For pregame NBA contracts, a fresh, exact ESPN fixture response can supply an Out or Suspended player input. The calculation compares at least three explicit DNPs with at least four games of 20 or more minutes. It subtracts expected results from a team-strength fit trained before the sample, shrinks the difference toward zero with a four-game prior and caps the shift at 12 percentage points. Each player is stressed separately. The forecast keeps its validated baseline and the paper entry and hold-or-sell checks use the union of the baseline and scenario ranges. This can withhold a recommendation when it no longer clears costs. The scenario has no injury-specific calibration or causal effect estimate.

## Film preference

The requested 75–80% critic emphasis is implemented as a user preference for an experimental display index, default 77.5%, with a 75/77.5/80 sensitivity view. It requires a provenance-bound exact-film critic observation with a sample count plus a same-territory demand percentile within 24 hours. Missing inputs are not fabricated, imputed or silently reweighted. No live critic/demand measurement feed was supplied, so current film coverage can be context-only.

[Rotten Tomatoes defines](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/about) critic-positive share separately from audience sentiment. Neither measure is ticket demand or the probability of a gross threshold. Presales, release footprint, audience scores and realized box-office gross remain separate observations. No claim that 75–80% is empirically optimal is made.

## Verification

`node --test tests/evidence.test.mjs tests/workspace-research.test.mjs tests/provider-failure.test.mjs` covers opt-in boundaries, source identity and date filters, heuristic Reddit rejection, robots/access stops, shared X cost reservations, provider backoff, source-unit mismatches, DNP/missing player handling and film preference limitations. Live results and source hashes are recorded separately in `evidence-verification.json`; they demonstrate observed reads, not future availability, predictive accuracy or trading performance.
