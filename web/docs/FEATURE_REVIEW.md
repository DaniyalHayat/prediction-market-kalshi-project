# Research and engineering upgrade review

Reviewed November 4, 2026 (America/New_York). The full requested feature list is tracked below. Implemented features use this repository and the existing Vercel, Railway and Supabase accounts. No new paid integration was purchased. A local benchmark is not an end-to-end latency or trading-return result.

## Forecasts, position decisions and portfolio mathematics

The default NBA probability remains the regularized team-strength model. A separately selectable challenger trains ridge logistic, depth-two XGBoost and a 16-by-8 neural network on actual historical scores. A selection partition chooses the classifier/blend, a separate partition fits beta calibration, and a final chronological partition evaluates it. The selected composition is 75% team strength and 25% lag-feature logistic before beta calibration. The neural network's non-convergence is recorded rather than silently discarded.

Across 514 held-out games, Brier error decreases from 0.2025336534 to 0.1993854751, a 1.554% relative point improvement. Log loss decreases from 0.5935887721 to 0.5860446437. The paired weekly 95% improvement interval is approximately [-0.0011701, 0.0075196]. This promising experiment is usable through the experimental model setting; its formal promotion gate remains false. The held-out results concern outcome forecasts, not historical entries, fills or realized returns. See `NBA_RESEARCH.md`, `nba-challenger-validation.json` and `nba-inference-benchmark.json`.

The experimental in-play NBA model conditions on an actual current score and clock, following Stern's score-difference diffusion. Its prior comes from an independent pregame model. Its score-variance scale is fitted from historical calibration games, not invented. It refuses unsupported overtime and missing/stale live state. `NBA_INPLAY.md` and `nba-inplay-validation.json` record the historical state replay, parameter estimation and limitations.

The project-specific mathematical composition is an auditable combination of established methods, not a claim to a newly proved theorem. The new elements in this application are their integration with exact event mapping, dated model residuals, current executable depth, explicit uncertainty and risk constraints, and the known-event exposure graph.

For a selected side with independent chance p, range [l,u], quantity q and all-in entry cost C(q), expected settlement profit is p*q-C(q). Its sensitivity range is [l*q-C(q),u*q-C(q)]. If B is the entered budget, winning and losing ending values are B-C(q)+q and B-C(q). The chance of reaching 2B is calculated from these two actual branches; if neither branch reaches the target, it is zero. This is a settlement calculation, not a fabricated price-path forecast.

Budget scenarios spend only what the current book and fees permit. A separate discrete Kelly optimizer maximizes the lower-bound expected log growth subject to a drawdown-moment condition. With lambda=log(beta)/log(alpha), it checks l*(W_win/B)^(-lambda)+(1-l)*(W_lose/B)^(-lambda)<=1. The displayed defaults alpha=0.8 and beta=0.1 are explicit risk-policy assumptions. A repeated-wealth guarantee would require correct stationary probabilities and repeated independent opportunities; those conditions are not claimed for this app.

The live paper tracker compares q*p with current net bid proceeds R(q). HOLD requires the lower value q*l to exceed R(q) plus a visible action buffer. EXIT requires even q*u to fall below current proceeds minus the buffer. Overlap produces REVIEW. TRIM applies an explicitly entered position-loss limit. A falling quote alone is not evidence that the original thesis failed. Partial depth, stale quotes or missing model evidence withhold full-position P/L or actions. Confirmed binary settlements are recorded separately from bid closes. Multiple open lots share one proportional allocation of their common bid book, with separately rounded lot fees; the portfolio cannot reuse the best bid or count unavailable liquidity twice. Wrong-contract packets, failed refreshes and pregame estimates after event start cannot issue current model actions.

Validated rankings require a positive lower-bound edge after the budget-sized fill and buffer. Experimental rankings allow positive point-estimate edges while showing the lower-bound risk and failed model-promotion gate. Every row uses the same entered budget as an alternative scenario; the app does not pretend that each row creates additional portfolio cash. The event-overlap graph prevents duplicate ranked exposure to a known common event. Unknown cross-event correlations remain unknown.

## Public trader research and source limits

Kalshi's official help describes an opt-in leaderboard. Public leaderboard pages show projected profit rankings, which differ from verified account-wide realized return. The public trade API does not establish every participant's identity, capital, hedges or private positions. The direct public leaderboard request returned HTTP 429 during this audit; the access limit was respected. No private profiles, messages or contacts were accessed.

The supplied-data tool `node tools/analyze_public_traders.mjs public-closed-trades.json analysis.json` accepts only explicitly public, source-linked, deduplicated closed-trade cash flows. It calculates all observed history, trailing seven days and trailing four calendar months. Fewer than 30 available traders produce fewer than 30 rows. Missing records never become zero trades. Public winner anecdotes and forum copy-trading claims suggest tests for concentration, specialization, latency and entry cost; they are not training labels or proof of a person's strategy.

## Cost and integration rule

The self-built calculation engine, MCP server, stream fanout, job queue, FTS search and logger run on the existing stack. The dated gateway catalog lists Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite at $0.10/M uncached input tokens and $0.40/M output; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at $0.195/M and $0.78/M; GPT-5 nano at $0.05/M and $0.40/M. Provider availability, regional routing and tool quality still require live verification. Gemini remains the currently verified route. Model computation does not use an LLM.

Google AI Pro can include a claimed Google Developer Program cloud-credit benefit, and eligible ChatGPT Pro 100 subscriptions can activate API credits for a linked organization. These are account benefits, not credentials that the website can automatically borrow from a signed-in consumer chat session. No unverified credit balance is included in this app's operating forecast. Existing app and platform quotas remain active. The report distinguishes installed features, tested local experiments, live verification and future optional provisioning.

The bounded live Gateway comparison made exactly four requests: one scoped tool-routing step and one grounded follow-up per model. Both Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite and GPT-5 nano passed both tasks. Gemini used 487 input and 123 output tokens, took 2.151 seconds across the two steps and cost $0.00009790; nano with minimal reasoning used 799 input and 179 output tokens, took 2.745 seconds and cost $0.00011155. Gateway-reported costs agree with the recorded token rates. Gemini remains the route because nano was 13.94% costlier and 27.58% slower in this tiny sample. This is not a general quality or p95 comparison and excludes remote MCP transport. `ai-gateway-benchmark.json` and the opt-in benchmark script preserve the exact requests, grading and costs.

Small predicted improvements remain experiment candidates. The partitioning experiment is an example of testing the hypothesis and keeping its negative result: the indexed heap was faster for that fixture. The row above records the present implementation and the evidence needed for a later replacement, rather than treating a product name as a performance result.

The existing single Railway backend was moved from SFO to Virginia without adding another service or volume. Seven pooled SELECT 1 samples per location measured mean database round-trip time of 55.30 ms before and 19.26 ms after, a 36.04 ms / 65.18% reduction in this short comparison. Applied configuration, the successful deployment manifest and a running instance confirm the destination region. This is a database-path measurement, not a whole-app speed claim. `deployment-latency.json` and `RUNTIME_RESEARCH.md` preserve the evidence and scope.
